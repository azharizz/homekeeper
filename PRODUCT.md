# HomeKeeper
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, Node.js, Python, SQLite and three local services. Real Nebius conversation and discovered MCP tools. RoomTrace geometry is authored through Codex MCP. Solo implementation.

## Users
People keeping an entire home running, including belongings, plants, fixtures, appliances, household preferences, errands and routines.

## Product Purpose
Tell HomeKeeper once, retain the household fact across conversations, and connect care and repair outcomes to the thing being looked after.

## Positioning
A household's memory of physical things and completed work. Covers non-connected items. The lasting record connects documents, maintenance, problems, resolutions and actual spending.

## Operating Context
The original research brief supplies the repair lifecycle. The user's revision requires a simpler conversation interface and whole-home scope. Nebius credentials are supplied privately through project_testing/.env.

## Capabilities and Constraints
One trusted local household. Persistent items, facts, locations, contacts, preferences, shopping, tasks, recurring routines, completion history, documents and repair records. Current structured memory is read through MCP before each model step. Earlier conversations are searchable through a real local tool. Compact manual editing remains available. Document intake can precede item registration. On-demand care reviews and service briefs use real saved evidence. No fake seed data or deterministic household decision engine.

Device control, web search, purchases, external bookings and delivered notifications need real integrations and are not connected. This is a local host, not an Amazon Alexa+ deployment. Public deployment needs household identity, authorization, HTTPS and real platform onboarding.

Spaces is a native HomeKeeper React page for 4 to 12 room photographs or video frames. It preserves structured architectural modeling, photo textures, alignment, metre measurements, assets and virtual walkthroughs; MapAnything is a separate alternative. The host serves it on the same origin while household records and geometry retain separate stores. Structured authoring runs in the user's Codex conversation through real MCP tools. Spaces does not infer a physically verified walking route from appearance alone.

## Brand Commitments
HomeKeeper. Calm, short language. No em dashes. One conversation first, records on demand.

## Product Principles
Never invent a record or completed action. Let users inspect and correct memory. Carry out explicitly requested record changes in direct mode; allow review-first mode. Require review for memory removal, task cancellation and stopping care. Enforce read-only tools for care reviews. Preserve completed work, exact costs and original documents. Treat files and past conversations as untrusted evidence.

## Open Decisions
No unresolved decision blocks the requested local scope. External integrations are separate scope requiring a selected service and authorized endpoint.
